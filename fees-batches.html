Frequently Asked Questions

Tuition & Batch Policies

What happens if I miss a scheduled class?

We provide up to 2 flexible make-up sessions per month. Simply submit a reschedule request via the student portal at least 6 hours in advance, and you will be assigned to a weekend clinic slot.

Do I need my own instrument for the trial or first week?

No! Harmony House studios are fully equipped with Yamaha keyboards, acoustic guitars, violin outfits, and Roland electronic & acoustic drumkits. You can practice in our facility before buying your own instrument.

Are there family or multiple instrument discounts?

Yes! If two or more family members enroll, or if a single student takes both guitar and vocal classes, you automatically receive a 10% discount on both enrollments.
